# The Cardiovascular System

## The heart

- Heart structure
    - Four chambers
        - Right atrium and right ventricle
        - Left atrium and left ventricle
    - Four valves
        - Tricuspid
        - Pulmonary
        - Mitral (bicuspid)
        - Aortic
    - Layers of the wall
        - Endocardium
        - Myocardium
        - Pericardium
    - Great vessels
        - Vena cava
        - Pulmonary artery and veins
        - Aorta

## Blood flow

- Pulmonary circuit
    - Deoxygenated blood enters the right atrium
    - Pumped through the tricuspid valve into the right ventricle
    - Sent to the lungs via the pulmonary artery
    - Oxygenated blood returns via pulmonary veins
- Systemic circuit
    - Oxygenated blood arrives in the left atrium
    - Passes the mitral valve into the left ventricle
    - Leaves through the aorta
- Coronary circulation
    - Left coronary artery and branches
    - Right coronary artery and branches
    - Cardiac veins drain into the coronary sinus

## Physiology

- Cardiac conduction
    - Sinoatrial (SA) node sets the pace
    - Atrioventricular (AV) node relays the signal
    - Bundle of His and Purkinje fibers
- Cardiac cycle
    - Systole: contraction and ejection
    - Diastole: relaxation and filling
- Blood pressure essentials
    - Systolic and diastolic readings
    - Cardiac output and peripheral resistance
